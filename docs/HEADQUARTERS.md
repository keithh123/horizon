# Headquarters campaign reference

GENERATED by scripts/gen_headquarters.py from `manifests/headquarters.json`. Design and state rules: docs/PROGRESSION.md.

> Long before the villages, a frontier survey company strung a line of observatories across the continent: the Lantern Line. Each station watched one horizon and sent what it saw to the next. The line went dark, the stations fell, and the maps were scattered. You carry the last charter of the Meridian Survey. Plant it anywhere, restore a station around it, and relight the line one horizon at a time.

## I. Outpost (`outpost`)

Plant the charter, sleep at home, store what you find, set up a survey table and walk to the nearest ruin.

*Survey log:* Station founded. The charter is planted, a bed is made and the first map table is cold but level. Somewhere close there is a ruin; the old line always passed near one.

- **Requires:** nothing (place a Survey Charter)
- **Commission at:** the bound Survey Charter (right-click it; act within 8 blocks)
- **Contribution consumed once:** 3x `minecraft:paper`, 8x `minecraft:torch`
- **Unlocks:** A copper Construction Stick, a timber-and-stone building kit and a Surveyor's Chart to the nearest temple or arcane site.
- **Services:** none
- **Physical expression:** Station Emblem I (`horizon:emblem_outpost`)
- **Next:** Workshop: press your first Survey Plates on a Create line.

Requirements:

- **Sleep in a bed within 24 blocks of the charter** (`home_spawn`, respawn_near) - proves: The commissioning player's respawn point is set within 24 blocks of the anchor.
- **Place 2 chests or barrels near the charter** (`storage`, block_any) - proves: Two storage blocks stand within the scan volume around the anchor at commissioning time.
- **Place a cartography table (the survey station)** (`survey_table`, block_any) - proves: A cartography table stands within the scan volume.
- **Plant a crop or breed animals (reliable food)** (`food`, adv_any) - proves: A member holds a vanilla husbandry advancement.
- **Step inside any roadside ruin, camp or settlement** (`first_ruin`, adv_any) - proves: A member has stood inside a structure of the roadside or settlement family (location advancement).

## II. Workshop (`workshop`)

Run a Create press line, press Survey Plates, add a construction station and bring back a find from a dungeon, temple or wreck.

*Survey log:* The press is turning. The first plates came off it true enough to survey with, and the station can build for itself again.

- **Requires:** outpost
- **Commission at:** the bound Survey Charter (right-click it; act within 8 blocks)
- **Contribution consumed once:** 4x `horizon:survey_plate`, 8x `create:andesite_alloy`
- **Unlocks:** An iron Construction Stick, Create's schematic tools, a millstone and fan for ore processing, and the daily Supply Crate service.
- **Services:** Supply Crate
- **Physical expression:** Station Emblem II (`horizon:emblem_workshop`)
- **Next:** Expedition Hall: prepare for, and finish, two of three regional expeditions.

Requirements:

- **Make sheets with a Mechanical Press** (`press_ran`, adv) - proves: Create's builtin trigger fires only when a press actually produces sheets (not on crafting or placing).
- **A Mechanical Press stands at the station** (`press_here`, block_any) - proves: A press is within the scan volume.
- **A depot, basin or belt feeds the press** (`line_here`, block_any) - proves: A feed block is within the scan volume.
- **Place a schematic table or crafting table (construction station)** (`construction_station`, block_any) - proves: A construction workstation is within the scan volume.
- **Enter a dungeon, temple, ocean ruin or arcane site** (`local_expedition`, adv_any) - proves: A member stood inside one of four common structure families.

## III. Expedition Hall (`hall`)

Furnish an equipment bench, an archive lectern and a discovery display, then finish two of three regional expeditions.

*Survey log:* The hall is open. The archive holds what the line knew about two regions, and the cases already hold something the old surveyors never saw.

- **Requires:** workshop
- **Commission at:** the bound Survey Charter (right-click it; act within 8 blocks)
- **Contribution consumed once:** 4x `minecraft:book`, 2x `minecraft:map`
- **Unlocks:** Warp scrolls and expedition gear; the survey desk keeps every regional chart you have contributed.
- **Services:** none
- **Physical expression:** Station Emblem III (`horizon:emblem_hall`)
- **Next:** Arcane Conservatory: commission one research room with Ars Nouveau, Iron's Spells or Occultism.

Requirements:

- **An equipment bench: smithing table, anvil or grindstone** (`equipment_bench`, block_any) - proves: An equipment workstation is within the scan volume.
- **An archive: a lectern or chiseled bookshelf** (`archive`, block_any) - proves: An archive block is within the scan volume.
- **A Discovery Case or any boss trophy on display** (`display`, block_any) - proves: A Horizon display block is within the scan volume.

**Regional expeditions: complete 2 of 3.** Finish any two. The third stays open as an optional adventure and still adds its charts later.

- **The Drowned Coast** (`coast`): Wrecks, sunken ruins and monuments. Bring a boat, Respiration and night vision. Contribution: 3x `horizon:tidebound_doubloon`, 8x `minecraft:prismarine_shard`. Tide charts: the survey desk can draw maps to ocean ruins and monuments.
  - **Enter an ocean structure** (`ocean_found`, adv) - proves: Location advancement for the ocean family.
- **The Hollow Roads** (`hollows`): Crypts, mines and mage towers under and around the hills. Bring a shield, torches and magic resistance. Contribution: 8x `irons_spellbooks:arcane_essence`, 2x `horizon:wayfarer_shard`. Archive charts: the survey desk can draw maps to temples and arcane sites.
  - **Enter an underground dungeon** (`underground_found`, adv) - proves: Location advancement for the underground family.
  - **Enter an arcane site** (`arcane_found`, adv) - proves: Location advancement for the arcane family.
- **The High Landmarks** (`highlands`): Great landmarks and the mini-bosses that keep them. Bring rare gear, healing and a way home. Contribution: 1x `horizon:ancient_core`. Landmark charts: the survey desk can draw maps to great landmarks.
  - **Enter a great landmark** (`landmark_found`, adv) - proves: Location advancement for the landmark family.
  - **Defeat any landmark mini-boss** (`keeper`, adv_any) - proves: A member holds a first-kill legend advancement (relics from it stay personal).

## IV. Arcane Conservatory (`conservatory`)

Commission one research or ritual room - Ars Nouveau, Iron's Spells or Occultism - and feed it an Ancient Core.

*Survey log:* The conservatory hums. Whatever the old surveyors used to see past the weather, we have something like it again.

- **Requires:** hall
- **Commission at:** the bound Survey Charter (right-click it; act within 8 blocks)
- **Contribution consumed once:** 1x `horizon:ancient_core`, 16x `minecraft:amethyst_shard`
- **Unlocks:** The Ward of Passage (resistance and fire safety for an expedition, once per day per member) and lair charts to boss arenas.
- **Services:** Ward of Passage, Lair Chart
- **Physical expression:** Station Emblem IV (`horizon:emblem_conservatory`)
- **Next:** Foundry and Logistics: power, assembly and networked storage.

Requirements:

- none beyond the choice below

**Research route: complete 1 of 3.** Any one route commissions the room; the others stay full side paths with their own rewards.

- **Source Study (Ars Nouveau)** (`ars`): Utility magic and automation: an Enchanting Apparatus at the station. Contribution: 8x `ars_nouveau:source_gem`. Source studies on the archive shelves.
  - **Craft an Enchanting Apparatus** (`apparatus_made`, adv) - proves: Ars Nouveau's advancement for the apparatus.
  - **The apparatus stands at the station** (`apparatus_here`, block_any) - proves: Apparatus within the scan volume.
- **Spell Forge (Iron's Spells)** (`irons`): Combat magic: an Arcane Anvil and an Inscription Table at the station. Contribution: 16x `irons_spellbooks:arcane_essence`. Inscription records on the archive shelves.
  - **Craft an Arcane Anvil** (`anvil_made`, adv) - proves: Iron's Spells advancement for the anvil.
  - **Arcane Anvil and Inscription Table at the station** (`forge_here`, block_any) - proves: Two Iron's workstations within the scan volume.
- **Spirit Circle (Occultism)** (`occultism`): Spirits and rituals: a golden sacrificial bowl and a working summoning. Contribution: 2x `occultism:chalk_white`. Spirit ledgers on the archive shelves.
  - **Complete a spirit summoning ritual** (`ritual_done`, adv_any) - proves: Occultism ritual advancements fire on a completed ritual.
  - **A golden sacrificial bowl at the station** (`bowl_here`, block_any) - proves: Bowl within the scan volume.
